What we cover

Five PI sub-practices.
One operating partner.

Famaash supports the full plaintiff PI book. Each sub-practice is operated by a Famaash team trained on its case-type taxonomy, its insurer playbook, and its medical record patterns.

01

Auto & MVA

Typical case profile

Rear-end, intersection, rideshare, and commercial vehicle. Sixty to eighty percent of most PI books.

How Famaash supports it

Multilingual intake in English, Spanish, Arabic. Insurer-specific demand letter templates. Medical lien negotiation desk.

02

Slip-Fall & Premises

Typical case profile

Retail, residential, and commercial property. Statute and notice requirements vary by jurisdiction.

How Famaash supports it

Notice-letter automation. Premises owner identification. Surveillance evidence triage.

03

Trucking & Commercial

Typical case profile

FMCSA implications, multi-defendant complexity, federal preemption questions.

How Famaash supports it

DOT records pull. ELD data preservation letters. Multi-state coordination.

04

Medical Malpractice

Typical case profile

Statute of limitations strict. Expert witness costs high. Certificate-of-merit requirements vary.

How Famaash supports it

Pre-suit screening. Expert witness coordination. Medical record indexing.

05

Premises & Workplace

Typical case profile

Slip-fall edge cases, third-party employer liability, workers compensation interaction.

How Famaash supports it

Comp-to-PI conversion analysis. Third-party tortfeasor identification.
