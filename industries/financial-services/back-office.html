The problem

Operations is where wealth practices fail at scale.

New-account paperwork takes nine days at the median. ACATs sit for two weeks. Performance reports go out late, with manual reconciliation patches that nobody enjoys writing. The advisor experience compounds the lead-advisor capacity problem one step downstream.

Hiring is the obvious fix and the slowest one. Specialised back-office staff, RIA-experienced, custodian-fluent, are scarce in any market.

Famaash provides credentialed back-office operators embedded in your custodian portals and CRM, working under our entity, measured against task cycle time.

How we run it

Four operations lanes, one accountable team.

New accounts, ACAT processing, beneficiary and account maintenance, and performance reporting, covered.
