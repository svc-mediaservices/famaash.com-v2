The problem

Advisor capacity is the only practice constraint that matters.

In a typical lead-advisor practice, eleven hours a week disappear into plan drafting, post-meeting follow-up, and CRM hygiene that should have been off the advisor’s plate years earlier. The capacity ceiling is rarely market demand. It is the calendar.

Hiring junior advisors solves the problem at five years and a six-figure cost per seat. A paraplanner layer staffed under a managed-services entity solves it at thirty days.

Famaash deploys credentialed paraplanners and meeting-prep operators directly into your CRM and calendar, returning advisor hours to client-facing work.
