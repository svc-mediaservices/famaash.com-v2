The problem

Delivery capacity should not be a hiring decision.

A consulting firm running ten cases at peak and four at trough hires analysts against the peak and pays for the trough. Or hires against the trough and quietly burns out the team during peak.

The healthier model is a flex bench, analysts available against the peak, retained outside the firm against the trough, with the same deliverable discipline either way.

Famaash operates the bench: hires the analysts, holds the QA bar, manages the staffing, and bills against utilisation, not headcount.
