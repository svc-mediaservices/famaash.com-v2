The problem

Research capacity is the chronic case-team constraint.

A boutique strategy firm with twenty consultants needs research capacity that flexes, sometimes one analyst-week, sometimes ten. Hiring against the peak leaves the firm carrying overhead. Hiring against the trough leaves cases under-staffed.

Outsourcing research to generic providers fails because the work fails partner review. The firm spends more time correcting research than it would have spent doing it.

Famaash provides partner-grade research analysts on a managed-services basis. Conflict-checked, MNPI-walled, and held to the same deliverable bar as in-house staff.
