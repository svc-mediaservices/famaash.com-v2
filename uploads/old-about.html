Thirteen years, four industries

The operating partner
built for operators.

Book your Audit →

Famaash was founded in 2013 by operators who’d run growth at McKinsey, BCG, and three of the four Big Four advisory practices.

We started as a B2B marketing firm serving enterprise consulting and Big Four clients. Our first invoice was paid by a Tier-1 strategy firm. Within two years, healthcare clients were asking us to build the product, not just the campaign. That became our engineering practice. Within five, financial services clients were asking for embedded teams under our entity. That became our talent practice.

Today we are the operating partner for plaintiff personal injury law firms, with practices across healthcare, financial services, and management consulting. Marketing, talent, and engineering are not departments. They are the practice areas that cut across every industry we serve.

We don’t compete on price. We compete on cost-per-outcome, and we report it quarterly in the systems our clients already use.

Founding chronology

Five phases.
One throughline.

  1. I
    Origin
    2013–2015

    Founded as a B2B marketing firm serving enterprise consulting and Big Four practices. First client was a Tier-1 strategy firm.

    —The first invoice paid for the second hire.
  2. II
    Engineering arrives
    2015–2018

    Added engineering practice when a healthcare client asked us to build the product, not just the campaign. First production system shipped to a regional health system.

    —Marketing campaigns turned into operating systems.
  3. III
    Talent practice opens
    2018–2021

    Launched talent practice (EOR staffing) when financial services clients asked for embedded teams.

    —We stopped competing with agencies, started competing with consulting firms.
  4. IV
    The PI specialization
    2021–2024

    Acquired anchor PI client. Built UPL-safe AI intake from first principles. Quarterly CPSC reporting becomes the standard practice deliverable.

    —We found the industry that needed every practice we run.
  5. V
    The operating partner model
    2024–present

    Reorganized around four industry practices. Cross-trained every practitioner on industry-specific compliance regimes. SOC 2 Type II underway.

    —Operating partner became the only label that fit.
