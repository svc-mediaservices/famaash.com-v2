The EOR Model

Famaash hires every operator under our entity, not yours.

That means no W-2 versus 1099 ambiguity, no payroll tax exposure on your side, no benefits administration, and no employment-law surface area for your firm. We carry the employer responsibility. You receive the work product.

If a member of your embedded team leaves for any reason, the lifetime replacement guarantee triggers. We replace them from a pre-certified bench. The replacement reads existing case notes on day one. Your throughput does not stop.

One monthly invoice covers wages, payroll taxes, employer-side benefits, certification maintenance, replacement bench, and continuous training. No setup fees, no severance fees, no recruiting fees, no ramp fees.
